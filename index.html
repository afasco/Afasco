A little about me

Science with practical purpose.

My approach combines laboratory investigation, applied research and knowledge sharing to address challenges across food quality, fisheries and biological resource use.

I currently serve as a Research Officer at the Nigerian Institute for Oceanography and Marine Research (NIOMR), where I contribute to research focused on the safety and quality of fish and fish products, preservation methods and the reduction of post-harvest losses.

My professional experience includes microbiological examination of fish, food products and water; microbial shelf-life assessment; sensory evaluation; and supporting research activities that improve fish processing and storage. I have also contributed to collaborative project coordination, technical reporting and the evaluation of improved fish-smoking technologies.

With academic training in microbiology and biotechnology, I am particularly interested in connecting applied microbiology with circular bioeconomy approaches—especially the conversion of agro-industrial and marine by-products into useful, value-added products.
